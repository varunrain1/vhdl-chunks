Difference Equation → Poles, Zeros & Coefficients

Enter the feed-forward (b) and feedback (a) coefficients of a linear difference equation. The tool derives H(z), finds poles & zeros, plots them on the unit circle, and shows the frequency response. A 3D view shows |H| along the unit circle (angle = frequency).

Difference equation

Form:
y[n] = b0 x[n] + b1 x[n-1] + … − a1 y[n-1] − a2 y[n-2] − …
(a0 is fixed to 1)

Unit circle: z = ejω. Angle = digital frequency. Poles (×) must stay inside the circle for stability. Zeros (○) on the circle create notches.
