Remember:
• y[n] = output signal (time domain)
• h[n] = impulse response (time domain)
• H(z) = transfer function (z-domain)
• Frequency response = H(ejω) on the unit circle

FIR lets you design h[n] directly because it is finite. IIR designs H(z) because h[n] is infinite.
